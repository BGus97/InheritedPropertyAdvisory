The Process

The Estate Exit Plan

01

Clarify the Situation

Understand the real estate situation, decision-makers, property status, and goals of all parties involved.

02

Evaluate the Property

Assess condition, market positioning, and what—if any—preparation makes sense before a sale.

03

Compare Your Options

Review realistic real estate paths: traditional sale, as-is sale, investor offer, or preparing the home before listing.

04

Build the Strategy

Align on the best approach given timing, condition, market conditions, and stakeholder priorities.

05

Execute the Sale

Handle all details of the transaction—from listing or off-market sale through a clean closing.
